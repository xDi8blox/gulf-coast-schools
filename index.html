01 — About

Two traditions, one education.

Gulf Coast is an Islamic Montessori school dedicated to both Western and Islamic education, in a secure, serene environment in Ojota. Children study a science-led academic programme alongside the Qur’an, and grow in character as much as in knowledge.

The Montessori approach shapes the early years: children learn by doing, at their own pace, with teachers who guide rather than lecture. That independence carries on through primary and into secondary school.

“Our hallmark is unlocking the hidden potential of disengaged or struggling learners, instilling self-confidence, academic independence and moral excellence.”

Gulf Coast Schools

02 — Curriculum

A blended curriculum,
taught as one.

  1. I

    Nigerian

    The national curriculum is the foundation, with secondary pupils prepared for WAEC and JAMB.

  2. II

    British

    Elements of the British curriculum add depth in literacy, numeracy and enquiry-based science.

  3. III

    American

    An American influence encourages discussion, projects and a broad, well-rounded education.

  4. IV

    Islamic Studies

    Tahfiz (Qur’an memorisation) and Tarbiyyah (moral and spiritual upbringing) run through every year.
